# Comparison and score rules

Prefer the account's confirmed targets and comparable completed periods. The original pack's undated industry ranges are not verified benchmarks and must not fill missing data.

Check currency, conversion definition, date range, attribution, campaign type, volume and conversion delay before comparing periods. Use an external benchmark only with its source, measurement period and applicable population. Explain limits.

## Optional Skill health rubric

This is a reporting rubric, not a Google score or a prediction. Omit a total score when essential conversion evidence is missing or a base phase is incomplete.

For each of the four base dimensions, score only when its evidence is available:

- Campaign performance: compare measured CPA or ROAS with the owner's stated target and explain any directional score.
- Search terms: assess relevance and conversion evidence within reported terms, naming the share of account spend represented.
- Keyword hygiene: assess reviewed overlap and relevance evidence, without assuming missing queries or absent negatives are harmful.
- Ad copy: assess documented message match and supported asset checks, without treating ad strength as a business result.

If a numeric score is useful, agree the thresholds with the user before scoring. Record those thresholds, the observed inputs and equal 25% weights for four complete base dimensions. Compute the total as their arithmetic mean. Do not invent thresholds, silently reweight missing dimensions, or add PMax into that total without a separately agreed rubric. Otherwise return qualitative observations and write score unavailable.
